80	How do I find a place?
81	Type in the search box in Explore, or filter by region and category. Tapping a marker on the map also opens the place's page.
82	How do the trip list and route work?
83	Tap “Add to my trip list” on a place's page. Your trip list in the Plan section puts the places in the shortest order and starts directions.
84	Can I use it offline?
85	You can download the map of your trip list or of a whole province to your phone. Place information is stored on the phone; writing reviews and uploading photos need internet.
86	How do I add a place that is not listed?
87	Suggest it with “Suggest a place” in Explore. Once the community approves it, it is added to the map and you get a notification.
88	Why doesn't my photo appear right away?
89	The first photos from new accounts are checked by a moderator before they are published. This usually doesn't take long.
90	I saw wrong information. What should I do?
91	Use the “Wrong information? Suggest a correction” link at the bottom of the place's page.
92	Who can see my travel journal?
93	Only you. If you turn on “Share by link”, anyone with the link can see it; your expenses are never shared.
94	How do I turn off notifications?
95	You can turn off the app's notifications in your phone's settings.
96	How do I delete my account?
97	Tap “Delete account” at the bottom of the My profile page. Your reviews, photos and all records are permanently deleted.
98	My question isn't here
99	Write to us from “Send feedback”; we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
